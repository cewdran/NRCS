1. Authorized question and honest timing of this specification

Use current NRCS files to describe observed Whipstone Annual soil-pH plot
trajectories and compare two fixed, simple point baselines in separate annual
target rounds. This is a retrospectively specified, unblinded diagnostic: source
outcomes and the accepted inventory were already viewed before this protocol.
The file/specification freeze precedes this new execution, not the historical
measurement of targets. No preregistration, untouched-test or prospective
validation claim follows from the execution-order prediction freeze.

The user authorized this next stage on 7 October 2026 after the source-preserving
measured-outcome inventory. Ten-year work is authorized as a project goal, but
the current files do not establish empirical numerical ten-year forecasts.
This package creates no decadal curves, scenarios, confidence/prediction
intervals, significance tests, causal effects, complex models or chosen winner.

5. Observation eligibility and complete audit

An eligible response has the exact frozen workbook form/scope/record/PDF/lab
pointer, a confirmed physical plot and matching confirmed key/actual date, no
source exclusion, and one finite recorded numeric pH that is uncensored,
unqualified and free of unresolved numeric/text conflict. Original numeric
representation is copied; no imputation, censor substitution, rescaling or
source-selected averaging occurs. Missing/nonfinite/qualified/conflicting values
are held with explicit reasons. A mismatched frozen source identity fails the
integrity gate rather than creating a new alias.

Perform uniqueness gates on all authoritative W_PH candidate source rows with
confirmed physical/date identity BEFORE finite-value inclusion. More than one
workbook row at a plot/event holds every row even if only one is finite. More
than one actual event for a plot in one annual round holds every row in that
plot-round even if another event is missing/censored. Do not silently choose
the finite row, choose a visit, deduplicate, combine labs or average a response.
Unexpected source/metadata changes fail closed; held rows remain visible.

Measured soil eligibility does not require 16S, PICRUSt2, positive microbial
reads, a primary microbial arm or a microbial depth-sensitivity flag. Identity
must still be explicitly confirmed in the accepted measured inventory. Optional
treatment/source plot-group labels are copied only from source metadata, may be
blank, and are never inferred from plot names or used as a model/eligibility gate.

observation_audit.csv preserves all candidate source rows, their dispositions
and any expected record with no source row. An absent source creates an audit
entry with blank observation_id, never an invented measurement. Eligible
observation_id equals the original WB inventory_row_id. Exact reason codes and
interfaces are frozen in RESPONSE_SPEC.json and OUTPUT_CONTRACT.json.

The audit may also copy accepted outside-panel rows and laboratory representations

8. Exactly two reported-scale baselines

PERSISTENCE predicts a known plot's latest eligible strictly pre-cutoff source
pH. With unique confirmed events there is no value-based tie resolution.

EQUAL_PLOT_TRAINING_MEAN first takes the arithmetic temporal mean of eligible
strictly pre-cutoff source pH within each confirmed physical plot, then averages
those plot means with equal plot weight over ALL eligible training plots.
Different counts of historical visits do not give a plot more cross-plot weight.
There is no time weighting, outcome tuning, treatment-specific mean or fitted
trend. The derived training means are baseline parameters, not replacement soil
measurements or aggregated plot-event responses.

Both methods are applicable only to known plots with at least one eligible prior
pH observation. A cold-start target is COLD_START_NO_PRIOR_PH for both methods,
even though a pooled mean might numerically exist. A training pool must contain
at least one eligible plot to define its mean. These existence conditions are
chosen design/support rules, not power, estimability or predictive-skill guarantees.
Arithmetic pH means are declared descriptive parameters on the reported pH scale;
they are not averages of hydrogen ion concentration/activity or soil-process
forecasts. No nonlinear chemical interpretation is attached to them.

9. Target ledger, errors and paired diagnostics

plot_errors.csv is a full target-score ledger for both methods for every declared
target record, including held/missing responses and cold starts. Score only a
unique eligible target actual event for a known physical plot with an available
finite frozen prediction. Record signed error = predicted_pH - reported_pH,
absolute error and squared error. Unexpected nonfinite derived parameters fail
closed rather than silently extending a skip rule.

performance.csv reports MAE and RMSE separately for each fold and method, with
equal weight per scored physical plot. paired_errors.csv uses the exact common
eligible target physical plots with both predictions; paired_performance.csv
reports the same two methods on precisely that shared set. Preserve per-method
and common-set sample support separately. Never compare incomparable sets while
calling them paired. n=0 means NOT_ESTIMATED and empty metric cells, not zero;
positive n yields ESTIMATED_POINT_DIAGNOSTIC, with no inferential guarantee.

cohort_support.csv and baseline_support.csv state the actual recorded training,
target, scored and common-set support, all computed on Puma. Never pool the two
folds as independent validation observations, rank a winner, tune against these
targets, calculate p-values/CIs or infer causal treatment effects from them.
Repeated observations share physical plots, and annual field/assay/crop contexts
are not independently replicated environmental conditions.

11. Interpretation and subsequent decision

Accept a completed stage only after source/manifest/runtime/execution integrity,
full ledger/paired-method consistency and scientific/visual review. A limited
point diagnostic can support observed-period manuscript description and guide
an independently frozen future sampling/assay/dependence/validation design.
It cannot by itself establish prospective skill or decadal calibration. Numerical
ten-year readiness still requires actual driver/management/scenario definitions,
measured starting-state/outcome harmonization, appropriate external/calibration
evidence, independent temporal validation and defensible uncertainty. None is
invented or presumed complete here. REFERENCES.txt records sources and their
limited methodological relevance; no literature supplies a universal visit count.
