8. Exactly two baseline methods, separately by response

PERSISTENCE predicts a known plot's latest eligible strictly pre-cutoff
original value for that same response. Unique confirmed events permit no
value-based tie resolution.

EQUAL_PLOT_TRAINING_MEAN takes the arithmetic temporal mean of eligible
strictly pre-cutoff values within each confirmed physical plot for that same
response, then the arithmetic mean of those plot means with equal plot weight
over ALL eligible prior training plots in that response. Unequal visit counts
do not give a plot more cross-plot weight. No future-conditioned pool, time
weighting, treatment-arm mean, tuning, regression, trend or process model.
These means are derived training parameters, not replacement measurements or
field plot-event response aggregation.

Both methods require a known target plot with at least one eligible prior
observation of that response. A cold start is COLD_START_NO_PRIOR_RESPONSE for
both methods even if a pooled mean exists. A training pool needs at least one
eligible plot to define parameters. Minimum-one gates are existence/design
rules, not power/estimability/predictive-skill guarantees. If a declared track
has no eligible training, preserve explicit no-estimate/support status and
False fitted/parameter/prediction flags for that track. Do not fabricate a fit
or map. An actually computed prior fit/map retains its True flag even when
future targets are absent/held or no target can score. Zero scored targets
yields NOT_ESTIMATED with empty metrics, never a zero-error estimate.
