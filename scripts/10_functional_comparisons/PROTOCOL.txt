Analysis units and scope

Oatman 2023-24 field; Oatman 2025 field; Page Springs Grenache; Page Springs
Petite Sirah; Whipstone Annual; Whipstone Chester; Whipstone Natchez. Physical

and visits do not increase the number of independent plots.
Primary scope: frozen primary-included positive non-reference samples with a
nonzero functional profile. Depth sensitivity: additionally require the existing
corrected depth_sensitivity_included flag. Do not introduce a new read-depth,
NSTI, function prevalence or statistical-significance selection threshold.
Retain original QC coverage/NSTI diagnostics, explicitly labelled as original
prediction diagnostics for the filtered variant. Zero profiles are logged and
held for their affected family. No imputation or rarefaction. No combining of
bacterial and archaeal library abundances.

Normalization and global composition
Normalize each sample to unit sum within its FULL EC, KO or MetaCyc profile,
then use square roots (Hellinger coordinates). No normalization over only the
curated panel. Descriptive PCA uses centered Hellinger coordinates, with all
primary samples displayed. A plot's observed visits are connected in date order.
No p-values or biological interpretation arise from ordination alone.

Global treatment associations
For each system/domain/scope/family and each version, consider the frozen exact
collection dates in CONFIG. A collection event enters treatment inference only
if EVERY declared arm has at least two independent physical plots. The same
all-arm gate controls targeted event contrasts; do not rescue a sparse T1 by
silently dropping it or testing selected other arms. All observations remain
in descriptive tables/figures and eligible paired temporal diagnostics.
This retains Petite Sirah 2025 T1, but withholds its standalone four-arm test.
Oatman 2025 treatment comparisons are wholly descriptive because treatment and
field side are confounded; its single event also has no paired temporal test.

Use one reduced model of actual collection-event indicators and one full model
adding treatment indicators. Give each plot total weight 1 (visit weight 1/v).
Response and design rows are multiplied by sqrt(weight). Report partial R2 as
(SSE_reduced-SSE_full)/SSE_reduced and a multivariate partial-F statistic. The
nominal row-based degrees of freedom scale the statistic only; never report an
F-distribution p-value or regard the visits as independent errors.
Permute treatment labels at WHOLE-PLOT level only within strata of identical
sorted actual visit-date patterns in the included events. Preserve every
plot's response sequence, weights, dates and treatment count per stratum.
Record all visit patterns, fixed versus movable labels, ranks, independent arm
counts and assignment-space size. No shared spatial blocks are inferred from
R1/R2/R3 replicate names. Skip aliased designs, no residual variation after
event adjustment, fewer than 20 distinct assignments or absent eligible events.
Zero full-model SSE can yield an infinite F; expose that flag and obtain its
p-value solely from permutations.
Exact unique multiset allocations when space <=5000, including the identity;
otherwise 4999 seeded Monte Carlo permutations with replacement and
p=(1+number_at_least_observed)/(1+4999). Equality tolerance is documented in
statistics_core.py. Report the assignment probability grid; ties/symmetry may
make the true minimum p larger. More random resamples do not create plots.

Fixed paired temporal comparisons
Use only these exact shared date pairs, never each plot's own first/last dates:
Oatman 2023-24: 2023-11-20 -> 2024-12-06;
Grenache: 2023-10-20 -> 2024-08-05;
Petite Sirah: 2023-10-20 -> 2025-10-10;
Whipstone Annual/Chester/Natchez: 2023-11-16 -> 2025-08-17.
Keep only physical plots observed at BOTH dates with constant treatment and
nonzero profiles. Log paired plots by treatment. Other date-pair observations
are descriptive only. Require at least three independent pairs for inference.
Global statistic: squared norm of the mean Hellinger-change vector. Whole-plot
sign flips preserve cross-function dependence; enumerate 2^n signs when <=5000,
otherwise use 4999 plus-one Monte Carlo flips. This assumes independent plots
and joint sign symmetry of each change vector under the null. The two-sided
sign symmetry makes the exact minimum p at least 2/2^n (ties may increase it).
Report mean-change norm, all individual Hellinger/Bray-Curtis paired distances,
pair count, dates and treatment mix. This is observed temporal association over
the retained plot mixture, not a causal treatment effect or a season-free trend.

Targeted associations and uncertainty
At each all-arm-eligible event, compare each declared noncontrol arm to its
prespecified control. One response per physical plot; >=2 plots in BOTH arms.
Statistic: absolute difference in mean FULL-table relative abundance. Permute
labels over the two independent arms, exact allocations <=5000, otherwise
4999 plus-one draws. Signed effect is treatment minus control. Sparse-event
contrasts and Oatman 2025 are explicitly skipped for inference.
For fixed shared dates, test the mean late-minus-early relative-abundance change
for each target across the matched cohort plot mixture, with whole-plot sign
flips and >=3 pairs. Treatment mix is reported; no treatment-by-time interaction
or unsupported treatment-specific temporal claim is fitted.
Return all per-sample values, event/arm means and observed minima/maxima. Return
approximate Welch or paired-t 95% intervals for scalar effects when sample
variance permits. These intervals rely on small-sample distribution assumptions
and have no guaranteed coverage for sparse/zero-heavy profiles. Degenerate
zero-width intervals are suppressed and flagged. P-values use permutation,
not Welch/paired-t distribution tests. Figure min-max bars are observed plot
ranges, not confidence intervals. No confidence interval is invented for global
R2. Report within-event distances to treatment centroids as descriptive spread
diagnostics; location/dispersion differences may be confounded with imbalance.

Multiplicity and archaeal robustness
Original and filtered archaea implement the SAME planned hypotheses and seed
schedule. Require identical nonzero-profile eligibility masks before any testing,
so the versions use the same eligible samples, events and paired physical plots.
Abort if eligibility differs; do not combine tests of different populations. Bacteria has one version. Retain every component result. A primary
archaeal robustness p is MAX(original p, filtered p); if either is untestable,
robust inference is untestable. Never select the lower p or preferred effect.
Apply Holm family-wise adjustment across all PLANNED unique GLOBAL hypotheses
(systems, domains, profile families and both treatment/paired-time test classes),
separately for primary and existing depth-sensitivity scopes.
For TARGETED results, freeze a family within each scope x system x domain x
response panel (ten ECs or four MetaCyc pathways) x test class. All declared
event/arm contrasts and dates are together within the event class; selected
features are together within the fixed-pair temporal class. This reflects distinct
biological systems/questions. The targeted family-wise guarantee applies only
within each listed family; there is NO project-wide targeted guarantee. Report
every family/effect and do not select positive families for broad study-wide
claims. Families are fixed before any new comparison results. This is not
hierarchical gatekeeping; targeted tests are not gated by global rejection.
Untestable planned hypotheses count as p=1
in adjustment and are displayed NA; they cannot receive support. Scalar
robustness also requires matching signed effect direction. Global support has
no single signed direction. Holm-adjusted conservative p<=.05 is a conditional
association label, not proof of a microbial process. Depth-sensitivity results
are secondary diagnostics; disagreement with primary must be disclosed rather
than resolved by picking a favorable branch. The report retains both scopes.

Interpretation and later work
These are exploratory conditional associations: field randomization and spatial
independence have not been established. Exchangeability/sign-symmetry assumptions
cannot be verified from a small field dataset. Treatment, season, weather,
irrigation, sequencing depth and management can affect these patterns. A null
result may reflect low independent replication and coarse attainable p-values.
In particular, 3 versus 3 plot two-sided scalar tests have minimum p=.10 and
2 versus 2 have minimum p=1/3; they cannot provide .05-level treatment support.
Those comparisons chiefly provide exploratory effect estimates and robustness
diagnostics. Short paired series can likewise have coarse or unattainable
corrected significance; absence of support is not evidence of no difference.
Predicted EC/pathway abundance is inferred relative genetic potential, not
measured genes, expression, enzyme activity, nutrient flux, yield benefit or a
quantitative nitrogen/carbon/phosphorus budget. This package does not fit soil-
microbiome models, forecasts, taxonomic reclassifications or ten-year scenarios.
Original diversity/richness/read-count results remain separate. Later integration
needs confirmed assay identities, a frozen small predictor set and eligible
model design. NIFA/NRCS sample reuse is still unresolved.
