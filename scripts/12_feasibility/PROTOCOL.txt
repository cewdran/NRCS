NRCS TEN-YEAR FEASIBILITY AUDIT - PROVENANCE PROTOCOL v2
Prepared 5 October 2026. Scientific execution is exclusively on Puma under Slurm.

Correction rerun scope
This version corrects two output-label defects identified while reviewing the
completed v1 Puma audit 24110010, run_20261005T221732Z_e5c3074d. That run remains
immutable. First, source-catalog hold flags were contaminated by empty keys
created during lookups in a defaultdict of held reasons. A retained S1 record
R0102 was correctly unheld in laboratory_record_catalog but falsely held in
laboratory_measurement_catalog. Second, a nonempty literal method placeholder
'Not specified on report' was classified as a recorded assay rather than an
unknown method requiring a hold. Neither defect authorized or changed a model,
statistical test, forecast, source value or main confirmed join: all approvals
in v1 remained false and main joins used a separate actual held-ID set.

The v2 run repeats the same descriptive source/design audit with corrected
catalog and assay annotations. Catalog hold flags and reasons must derive from
the authoritative corrected held-record IDs; reading an absent reason must not
create membership. Preserve literal source method text, including unknown-method
placeholders, but classify missing, unknown, conflicting or partially recorded
methods as unresolved/held. A reported variable name is not proof of assay
compatibility. Preserve the frozen sources, fixed candidate panel, corrected
identities, exclusions and scope. This version is not a harmonization pass,
approved within-study model, validation analysis or ten-year projection.

Purpose and authorization
Implement the feasibility stage of the approved planning extension in
NRCS_Workflow_Status_20260930_v3.pdf, version 2, pages 5-7. The approved sequence
is: small within-study response models where estimable; earlier-event training
and later-event validation; and, only with additional evidence, externally
calibrated conditional ten-year scenarios. This package performs the preceding
source/design audit. It does not fit response models, run forecasts, simulate
ten-year effects, claim validation, or establish external calibration.

Outcome inventory and candidate plan
Inventory every corrected measured outcome, keeping farm, biological system,
dataset, exact variable identifier, source assay/method and unit distinct.
OUTCOME_PLAN.csv names candidate response tracks and a small illustrative panel;
its rows are CANDIDATES or QC_ONLY, not approved numerical model outcomes.
Retain all inventory rows, including sparse, held, composite, censored, reference,
missing-unit and missing-date records. Presence in an inventory does not authorize
inference. A later immutable model protocol must freeze the exact response IDs,
cohort, transformations, predictors, contrasts, multiplicity and alternatives
after this audit is reviewed. Do not select targets because earlier tests were
significant, replace an absent target with a favorable one, or import earlier
CLR/FDR defaults without a newly declared analysis protocol.

Microbial candidates are Shannon, observed ASV richness, community structure and
the previously fixed ten EC/four MetaCyc targets. Bacteria and archaea remain
separate responses. Preserve original and filtered archaeal functional versions
and their matched population; inferential claims later require declared
sensitivity handling. Functional values are inferred relative genetic potential,
not measured genes, activities, fluxes or nutrient budgets. Read counts remain
sequencing-depth QC/covariate candidates, never absolute microbial load and never
a biological ten-year outcome. Community summary definitions and depth handling
remain a later modeling choice; do not invent extra derived responses here.

Measured candidates include verified soil variables, crop biomass, reported
grain yield and grain composition. Keep Haney, Ward and IAS assays distinct.
Organic matter by loss on ignition is not soil organic-carbon stock; organic
carbon is not active carbon; missing active-carbon/ACE-protein measurements are
not reconstructed. Yield retains its reported unit and unverified harvest-area
or conversion assumptions. Crop plot-season identity must be confirmed separately
from a soil aliquot or soil-16S match. Grain records with a year-only event cannot
be assigned a fabricated collection day. Petiole/juice variables, if present,
remain subject to their own matrix, identity, method and timing gates.

Missing fields are reported as missing; never fill them from similar records.

The HPC audit should provide: (1) source verification/disposition; (2) outcome
and plot/event support with actual dates and treatment counts; (3) duplicate,
technical, composite/reference and censored-record holds; (4) exact confirmed
relationship matches and unmatched records; (5) chronological validation-design

external-driver/calibration evidence checklist. Summaries of recorded support
are audit results, not fitted models. Figures, if any, are support diagrams or
recorded-data displays generated on Puma, not projected trajectories.

Relationship and assay gates
An explicit confirmed sample key with an eligible source disposition supports
identity only. It does not establish compatible assays, independent replication,
model rank or causality. Audit corrected confirmed match tables alongside
functional confirmed_join_audit and source decisions. Do not generate a join by
parsing a candidate key, matching only year, choosing a nearest date, assuming a
submission date is a collection date or matching a pooled specimen to every
member plot. For each proposed soil/crop/microbe relationship report timing,
matrix, physical unit, match evidence, dependence, units and all held reasons.
Compatible-looking variable names never justify cross-laboratory pooling.
Literal method placeholders such as 'Not specified on report' record that the
method is unknown; their nonempty text does not establish a known assay. Keep
that provenance text visible and the compatibility gate held. Future source-
driven harmonization must resolve exact analytical methods and units separately
from identity confirmation before pooling year-specific response records.

Within-study model feasibility: audit only
Report how many independent plots and genuinely dated events support each
response, observations per plot, treatment changes, arm sizes, missingness,
method discontinuities and treatment/date/field confounding. Visit counts are
necessary descriptors, not power calculations or an automatic eligibility pass.
A later small predictor set must have identifiable treatment/time/driver effects
and an estimable dependence structure. Plot intercepts/slopes are not assumed
estimable from sparse records. A fit, converged optimizer or nonzero rank does
not by itself establish a scientifically interpretable or generalizable model.

Chronological validation feasibility: audit only
Inventory possible earlier-training/later-testing event cut points using actual
response dates, distinguishing known-plot forecasting from transfer to new plots
or farms. Report whether the same physical plots and compatible outcomes recur,
whether proposed predictors would be available at the forecast origin, and how
many later occasions could supply independent temporal validation. Year-only
crop events and different fields are explicit obstacles. This package generates
no predictions and evaluates no predictive errors, coverage or skill.

Later validation must fit imputation, scaling, feature selection and ordination
within training data; preserve plot/time dependence and prevent future-response
or future-driver leakage. Compare against persistence and appropriate simple
treatment/season baselines with uncertainty in error and interval coverage.
Insufficient later occasions remains VALIDATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED even if a model
fits. Known-plot performance is not new-plot/farm transfer performance. Near-term
skill is not validation of a ten-year forecast horizon.

External long-term calibration and driver gates: not completed here
Do not browse, download or claim external calibration in this correction audit.
Preserve evidence present in frozen source catalogs; label unassessed evidence
NOT_ASSESSED_IN_THIS_RUN without inferring its global absence. A future numerical long-term
branch needs a suitable process model or transferable long-term field calibration,
actual starting states, documented management and driver histories, a defined
future climate/water distribution and scientifically defensible parameter and
boundary conditions. Depending on the process, these include soil texture,
sampling depth, bulk density for stocks, crop/rotation history, irrigation,
fertilizer/amendment amounts and timing, tillage/fire and water constraints.
Assay equivalence, climatic/soil/crop transferability and calibration independence
must be reviewed, with external sources and versions recorded. Literature
functions or pathway annotations are not calibration of this field's response.
MDSINE-type interaction models require stronger temporal information and microbial
load data; target sequencing reads do not supply microbial loads. No such model
is authorized merely because a microbiome series exists.

Ten-year scenario and uncertainty review gates
Every prospective outcome needs an explicit last eligible observed origin and
calendar end date ten years later; compute these from actual dates on Puma only
where identity/date precision permits. This defines a requested horizon, not
validated prediction skill. Future scenarios may be continued management under a
specified climate distribution, greater water limitation or a precisely specified
management change. Do not invent management rates or future weather. Numerical
scenarios require joint review of identifiable models, demonstrated short-horizon
performance, transferable external long-term evidence, plausible drivers and
uncertainty propagation. Carry initial-state, observation/assay, parameter, driver,
model and transfer uncertainty; distinguish within-scenario uncertainty from
differences between scenarios. Wider bands cannot validate unsupported
extrapolation. Allow stable, improving and deteriorating responses.

Audit gate schema (multiple held reasons may apply)
METADATA_SUPPORTED_REVIEW_PENDING: recorded identities/design support further
review; this never authorizes modeling or numerical projection.
HELD_IDENTITY: collection/plot/matrix/join identity or assay/units not resolved.
HELD_REPLICATION: independent units or arm support inadequate for the proposed
contrast; report the proposal, not a universal visit threshold.
DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY: reference/composite/confounded/one-event evidence permits only
the explicitly stated descriptive question.
VALIDATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED: useful earlier-to-later validation has not been
demonstrated. A potential split is not successful validation.
EXTERNAL_CALIBRATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED: long-term external calibration/process
support, starting states or plausible drivers have not passed review.
CONCEPTUAL_ONLY: numerical ten-year projection is unsupported; use literature-
grounded conditional hypotheses after separate literature review.
QC_ONLY: sequencing depth or other diagnostic, not a biological forecast outcome.
Candidate-plan status is CANDIDATE_AUDIT_ONLY or QC_ONLY. Model/validation/external
calibration status must remain NOT_PERFORMED/NOT_ESTABLISHED in this package.

Completion and manuscript use
Success means the auditable inventory and gate evidence were produced on Puma.
It does not grant a numerical model, forecast, external calibration or manuscript
scientific approval. The next model/protocol decision follows review of this
audit. Until all numerical gates are supported, retain a conceptual branch with
testable functions and stable/improving/deteriorating alternatives, not ten-year
effect sizes. Oatman may address nutrient turnover/organic matter and eligible
grain links; Page Springs management pathways and nutrient availability with
matrix/join holds; Whipstone within-system nutrient cycling; the cross-farm paper
compares equivalent within-system questions without treating three farms as
independent replication of crop, location or management effects.
