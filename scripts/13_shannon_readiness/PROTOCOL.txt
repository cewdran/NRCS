1. User purpose, work authorization and empirical scope
The user requests predictive validation and ten-year forecasts and specifies use
of current NRCS files only. Work toward those goals is authorized. Historical
permission/approval fields in earlier packages are retained as source history;
they do not negate the current authorization. Authorization is distinct from
empirical support: the existing files do not establish independent prospective
predictive validation, transferable long-term calibration, future driver paths
or calibrated long-horizon uncertainty. This package computes the next defensible
retrospective check and records what remains unsupported.

All scientific source preprocessing, count/response verification, membership
and eligibility checks, synthetic scientific fixtures, baseline fits,
predictions, scoring, readiness assessment and scientific reporting/figures run
only on genuine Puma compute nodes under Slurm. Local work is static code review,
syntax/packaging, hashes and editorial status diagrams. No scientific dry run,
fixture execution, matrix import or eligibility recomputation is permitted locally.

6. Three frozen point baselines, training-only support
PERSISTENCE: use the latest eligible training Shannon for that same plot.
EQUAL_PLOT_TRAINING_MEAN: first average eligible training visits within each
physical plot, then average those plot means equally over all eligible training
plots. For the new fold the allowed history is the single 2023 date.
EQUAL_PLOT_TRAINING_ARM_MEAN: average those plot means equally within the exact
source arm. All four arms must be present, their training physical-plot counts
equal, and each have at least two plots. Otherwise skip the entire arm method
with its existing explicit unbalanced/thin-support reason. No partial arms,
pooled arm, global fallback under the arm label or fitted treatment coefficient.
Exact arms: Blood_meal_and_Feather_meal;
Blood_meal_and_Feather_meal_and_cover_crop; Control; Cover_crop_only.
These are chosen conservative baseline support conditions, not assurances of
power, precision, adequate temporal coverage or biological transportability.

Serialize training memberships, support, plot means and predictions before
held-out Shannon enters scoring; retain training/prediction hashes. No target
preprocessing, imputation, tuning, depth covariate, interaction, random slope,
complex model, trend fit or winner selection is included. Known-plot history is
intentionally used; this is not leave-one-plot-out, new-field or farm transfer.

7. Evaluation within one annual round only
One equal weight per evaluated physical plot. Preserve observed/predicted values,
signed error (predicted minus observed), absolute/squared errors, actual date,
arm, sample/plot IDs, scope, origins, membership and n evaluated plots.
MAE and RMSE are computed for each fold/domain/scope/method separately.
Compare estimable methods only on their exact common evaluated plots with
identical observed response/date. Preserve fixed method fits when choosing a
common comparison cohort. PRIMARY versus DEPTH comparisons use their respective
unchanged training fits and a matched target intersection; do not interpret
this as a pure depth-adjustment effect. Record noncomparability and empty sets.

Report 2024 and reused 2025 results separately. The rounds reuse known plots;
2024 observations also enter the original 2025 training history. They are not
independent validation replicates. Do not pool their plot errors into an
independent n, estimate a general temporal error distribution, select a winner,
claim causal treatment/time/crop effects or compute intervals/p-values. No
bootstrap, inferential significance label or prediction interval is generated.
Actual date subsets remain documented provenance within one 2024 round.
